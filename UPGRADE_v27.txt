G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 27 (2026-11-03)
=========================================================================================

Barangay Contracts now follows the company's Standard Operating Procedure, and every
form and proforma has a configurable format (like the passbooks of WaterBilling).

This package holds ONLY the files that are new or changed since version 26, in the
same folders. Copy them over your installation (folder government-contracting-system
or accounting-system); every other file stays as it is.

Upgrading an installation of version 26
---------------------------------------
1. Back up the database and the program folder (storage/ and config/config.php too).
2. Copy the files of this package over your installation. config/config.php is not
   in the package; yours stays.
3. Run the new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
   Navicat: Execute SQL File):

       database/migrations/2026_11_03_barangay_sop_forms.sql

   It needs 2026_10_29_barangay_contracting.sql (version 26) to have been run first.
   With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies >
   Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page
   naming it.
4. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the new assets/js/brgy.js and the
   changed assets/css/app.css.

The migration adds 51 columns to brgy_projects, brgy_project_lines, brgy_entities,
brgy_templates, brgy_template_versions and brgy_forms, and five tables: brgy_sop_steps,
brgy_sop_papers, brgy_project_steps, brgy_project_papers and brgy_canvass. It adds the
page Barangay Contracts > SOP Steps & Papers and the report Transaction Monitoring (SOP)
with the rights of the contracting roles, the 30 steps and 40 papers of the SOP, 19 kinds
of documents, 24 templates, the number series TF- of the transaction form and the SOP
settings. No column is changed or dropped, nothing is deleted and no amount changes;
the projects already recorded keep the twelve phases. Written for MySQL 5.5 (every
column is added through a check of information_schema), safe to run twice.

New installation
----------------
Use the full version 26 package, copy this package over it, then import ONE of the two
database files of this package in phpMyAdmin (Import):

    database/government_contracting_system.sql        demo company with data
    database/government_contracting_system_clean.sql  setup and users, no transactions

Both already include this version. Log in as admin / Admin@12345 (a new password is
asked at once). Do NOT import those two files over a live database: they drop every
table.

What is new
-----------
 1. The SOP on every transaction. A new transaction chooses its process:
      - Small value procurement (the SOP's "below 100K"): 1 receive the transaction
        details (transaction form), 2 prepare the barangay's papers (PR, PO, IAR, RIS,
        canvass forms, abstract, DV), 3 supplier canvassing (three quotations with
        their signed forms), 4 billing documents (SI, OR, 2307), 5 review,
        6 file and monitor, 7 forward to accounting, 8 transmittal, 9 release.
      - Bidding: the 15 steps of the bidding process (bidding details, completeness,
        PhilGEPS posting, pre-bid papers, authorized representative, legal papers,
        eligibility papers, additional requirements, organize and label, signatures,
        final verification, submission, post-bidding papers, completion, payment
        schedule) and the 6 steps of the payment process flow.
      - The twelve phases of version 26, without the SOP.
    The Process tab of the transaction shows each step with what the SOP says, its
    papers and checks; a step is done only when nothing is missing, in order (an
    approver may skip a step or open it again, with the reason). Each step done goes
    to the timeline and the audit trail.
 2. The company prepares the barangay's papers: Prepare makes each one from its
    proforma, filled from the transaction (items, amounts, officials, taxes withheld,
    the seals of the barangay and the city on the letterheads); they print clean,
    without a review, blank fields as blank lines.
 3. Signed copies: every paper takes its signed copy as a PDF, JPG or JPEG file, filed
    in the Document Vault under the project ("Signed copies" tab), with older versions
    kept; Download signed copies gives all of a transaction's papers as one ZIP.
 4. Special case F: the SI, OR and 2307 are held while the payment is not released;
    follow-ups are logged, and Cheque ready records the cheque. The canvass records
    the award (or the loss), the signed IAR records the delivery and acceptance, and
    the payment goes to the books as before (VAT 5% and EWT 1% withheld on the amount
    net of VAT).
 5. Configurable forms. Every template (the 24 new ones and the 31 of version 26) has
    a Format tab with a live preview: paper (A4, Letter, Folio, Legal, A5, half letter
    or a size of your own), margins, font and sizes, line thickness and padding, blank
    rows, "nothing follows", the columns of each item table (shown, title, width,
    alignment), the parts printed, copies with their labels, printer offset, footer.
 6. Pre-printed paper: a template can print only its values on the barangay's pads,
    each field placed in millimetres over a scan of the paper (the scan never prints),
    with a test print on a millimetre grid. Formats and positions are kept with each
    version of the template.
 7. Barangays & Procuring Entities: the seals of the barangay and of the city /
    municipality, the officials the new forms use (Chairman, Committee on
    Appropriations; bookkeeper; city / municipal accountant), and the "ABC and Aboitiz
    requirements" switch for the offices that ask for them.
 8. SOP Steps & Papers (Setup): the steps, checks and papers of the SOP, as a flow,
    editable by approvers. Contracting Settings has a new SOP card.
 9. Report Transaction Monitoring (SOP): each transaction from its transaction form to
    the signed transmittal. The Contracting Dashboard shows the SOP transactions by
    step, and those waiting for payment.

Files
-----
 - storage/contracts/ also holds the signed copies, the seals (seal_*) and the scans of
   pre-printed paper (tplbg_*). Include it in your file backups. The files whose names
   start with d2026 are sample papers of the demo database; delete them on a live
   installation.
 - php tools/test_brgy.php --yes now also checks the steps and papers of the SOP and the
   formats of the forms (on a TEST COPY of a database only).

The user's manual, chapter 11, has the new sections The company's SOP, Format,
Pre-printed paper, SOP Steps & Papers and Transaction Monitoring (SOP).
