G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 37 (2026-11-24)
=========================================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_GOVERNMENT_CONTRUCTING_SYSTEM_v5.xlsx:
 1. The estimates and targets of the transaction form worked out as the sample transaction.
 2. The omnibus sworn statement as a whole form, like the other forms.
 3. The bid securing declaration as a whole form, like the other forms.

This package holds ONLY the files that are new or changed since version 36, in the same
folders. Copy them over your installation (folder government-contracting-system); every
other file stays as it is.

Upgrading an installation of version 36
---------------------------------------
1. Back up the database and the program folder (storage/ and config/config.php too).
2. Copy the files of this package over your installation. config/config.php is not in the
   package; yours stays.
3. Run the new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
   Navicat: Execute SQL File):

       database/migrations/2026_11_24_estimates_sworn_statements.sql

   It needs 2026_11_23_customer_zip_treasurer.sql (version 35) to have been run first.
   With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies >
   Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page
   naming it. It is safe to run a second time.
4. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.
5. Barangay Contracts > Contracting Settings > The company on the forms: fill in the
   representative in the sworn statements (civil status, citizenship, residence, the ID
   shown to the notary and its number, where the statements are signed and notarized).
   Left blank, they are blanks to fill on each form.
6. Drafts made from the old shells (BFM-00028 and BFM-00029 in the demo): open the draft
   > Edit; the page says the template has changed. Click "Fill again from the records".

What the migration does
-----------------------
 - One empty column on brgy_projects: est_sop_rate DECIMAL(5,2) (after est_sop), the SOP
   as a % of the gross amount. Blank: the SOP is the amount in est_sop, as before.
 - The templates "Omnibus sworn statement (prescribed form)" and "Bid securing declaration
   (prescribed form)" get a new version: the whole form (text, header, footer and affiant),
   category Proforma, no longer marked prescribed, Legal paper. The version before is kept
   (Proforma & Form Templates > the template > Versions; "Put back" restores it).
 - One line in the audit trail saying the migration was run.
Nothing is deleted, no amount changes and nothing is posted to the books. Forms already
prepared keep their text.

What is new
-----------
1. Projects & Contracts > New transaction / Edit > Transaction form > Estimates and targets
   The sample transaction of the Excel file (purchase order 50,000.00):
     Withholding tax    gross / 1.12 x rate          50,000 / 1.12 x 6%  =  2,678.57
     Cheque amount      gross - withholding tax                             47,321.43
     SOP                % of the gross, or an amount 50,000 x 2%         =  1,000.00
     Profit             gross x rate                 50,000 x 15%        =  7,500.00
     Receipt deduction  80% of the gross x rate      50,000 x 80% x 3%   =  1,200.00
     Amount to release  gross - tax - SOP - profit - receipt deduction      37,621.43
 - SOP: choose "amount" or "% of gross" beside its label. A % shows its amount under the
   box. Saved either way; editing shows it as it was typed.
 - The boxes work out as you type; the Process tab (step 1) shows the same figures, with
   the SOP rate, and the amount to release last.
 - Contracting Settings > The SOP: "Withholding tax: the gross divided by" (1.12; 1 for a
   supplier that is not VAT-registered) and "Receipt deduction: % of the gross" (80).
 - Transactions saved before keep their gross amount, rates and SOP; their figures are
   worked out the new way.
 - The printed transaction form takes the new figures; new field {{txn.sop_rate}}.
 - Fixed: a transaction whose bid was already submitted or awarded could not be saved
   again from Edit ("The abc amount cannot change once the bid is Submitted"), because
   the ABC sent back by the form (300000.00) was compared as text with 300000. It is now
   compared as an amount, so the estimates of an awarded transaction can be changed.

2. and 3. Forms & Proformas: the omnibus sworn statement and the bid securing declaration
 - Whole forms under RA 12009 and its IRR, as in the samples of the Excel file: the venue,
   the eleven items (omnibus) or the three (declaration) with a) to d), IN WITNESS WHEREOF,
   the affiant, the jurat of the notary public (Doc. No., Page No., Book No., Series of the
   year).
 - Filled from the records: the affiant (the authorized representative) and his details
   (Contracting Settings), the bidder (the tradename of the transaction, else the default
   tradename) and its address, the representative for the bidding (authorized submitter),
   the project title and PR / RFQ / ITB no., the barangay, city and province.
 - Changed like the other forms, on Proforma & Form Templates > the template:
     Text           Edit the text (or on each form, for that form only)
     Header         Format > Letterhead and logos (its own letterhead: the title over a rule)
     Footer         Format > Copies and printer > Footer
     Affiant(s)     Format > Signatories (another affiant can be added)
     Venue, jurat   Format > Parts printed (can be hidden)
 - New fields for any template: {{company.bidder}}, {{company.bidder_upper}},
   {{signatory.civil_status}}, {{signatory.citizenship}}, {{signatory.residence}},
   {{signatory.id_type}}, {{signatory.id_no}}, {{company.notary_place}}, {{date.year}}.
 - A draft whose template changed since it was made says so on its Edit page, with
   "Fill again from the records"; filled again, it takes the template's paper and is no
   longer a prescribed form.
 - The bid form stays a prescribed form (not asked to change).
 - In item 3 b) of the declaration the sample's line ends "...filed a waiver to avail of
   said"; the form ends it "...to avail of said right;" as the GPPB form does.

The User's Manual (Barangay Contracts > Projects & Contracts > Estimates and targets,
Forms & Proformas, Contracting Settings) explains it, with new pictures.
