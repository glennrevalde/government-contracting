G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 34 (2026-11-21)
=========================================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL MODULE FOR GOVERNMENT CONTRUCTING SYSTEM v4:
 - the card "Numbers and dates of the barangay's papers" taken off the transaction form;
 - People Accountable Set-up: each function set by ROLES or by USERS;
 - the transaction form: gross amount, withholding tax, cheque amount, amount to release,
   profit, receipt deduction and SOP (estimates and targets: no accounting entry);
 - the purchase request items (price schedule): "Look up the Inventory List", the estimated
   amount worked out (qty x estimated unit cost), add 1, 5 or 10 lines at once, any number
   of lines;
 - the IAR: RIS No. and Date beside the IAR No. and Date; no Requisitioning Office/Dept.,
   no Responsibility Center Code;
 - the Request for Quotation replaced with the barangay's format (header, footer, logos and
   text still changed on its template);
 - the DV: gross amount, VAT (5% / 3%) and EWT (2% / 1%) in its Fill in the blanks, the check
   amount and the amount in words worked out at once;
 - "Cheque ready: payment released": the tradename of the cheque, from every tradename.

This package holds ONLY the files that are new or changed since version 33, in the
same folders. Copy them over your installation (folder government-contracting-system);
every other file stays as it is.

Upgrading an installation of version 33
---------------------------------------
1. Back up the database and the program folder (storage/ and config/config.php too).
2. Copy the files of this package over your installation. config/config.php is not
   in the package; yours stays.
3. Run the new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
   Navicat: Execute SQL File):

       database/migrations/2026_11_21_people_users_estimates_dv_rfq_iar.sql

   It needs 2026_11_20_tradenames_people_report_center.sql (version 33) to have been run
   first. With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin:
   Companies > Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows
   a page naming it. It is safe to run a second time.
4. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the changed assets/css/app.css and
   assets/js/brgy.js.
5. Open Proforma & Form Templates > Request for quotation (canvass form) > Format >
   Letterhead and logos, and upload the Bagong Pilipinas logo as Picture A (the right side
   of the BAC letterhead). The barangay seal on the left comes from each barangay's record
   (Barangays & Procuring Entities), as before.
6. Check Contracting Settings > The SOP: the rates offered by the estimates of the
   transaction form (withholding tax 1,4,5,6,7; profit 10,13,15; receipt deduction 3).

What the migration does
-----------------------
 - The table brgy_function_users (People Accountable Set-up by user). Nothing is ticked:
   every function keeps the roles set in version 33.
 - Ten empty columns on brgy_projects: lines_lookup_inventory (the look-up switch of the
   price schedule), est_gross, est_wt_rate, est_profit_rate, est_receipt_rate, est_sop
   (the estimates), dv_gross, dv_vat_rate, dv_ewt_rate (the amounts entered on the DV) and
   cheque_tradename.
 - A new version of the template "Request for quotation (canvass form)" in the barangay's
   format, and of "Inspection and acceptance report (IAR)" with the RIS No. and Date and
   without the two lines. The version before stays under the template's Versions and can be
   put back. An IAR whose text was changed so that the two lines are not found is left as
   it is.
 - One line in the audit trail saying the migration was run.
Nothing is deleted and no amount or posting changes. Forms already prepared keep their
text; a draft takes the new format with "Fill again from the records". The numbers and
dates of the barangay's papers saved before stay on the transactions and still print.

What is new
-----------
People Accountable Set-up (Barangay Contracts > Setup)
 - Each function (sales representative, preparer, checker / reviewer, approver, authorized
   submitter, project manager, collector) has Roles or Users. Roles: the active users of the
   roles ticked. Users: only the people ticked by name. Nothing ticked: every active user.
 - Saving a function with Users clears its roles, and with Roles clears its users.
 - Choosing anyone else on the transaction form is refused, naming who is allowed.
The transaction form (Barangay Contracts > Projects & Contracts > New transaction)
 - No card "Numbers and dates of the barangay's papers": the numbers and dates of the PR,
   PO, RIS, IAR and DV are filled in on each paper (Fill in the blanks).
 - Estimates and targets, between the Fund and the Purpose:
     Gross amount        typed in
     Withholding tax     1%, 4%, 5%, 6% or 7% of the gross (its amount shown under it)
     Cheque amount       gross less withholding tax (worked out)
     Amount to release   gross less withholding tax (worked out)
     Profit              10%, 13% or 15% of the gross (its amount shown under it)
     Receipt deduction   3% of the gross (its amount shown under it)
     SOP                 an amount typed in
   No accounting entry is made from them. Step 1 of the Process tab shows them, and the
   printed transaction form takes its gross, withholding tax, cheque amount and profit from
   them (the cheque actually released and the taxes actually withheld come first).
The price schedule (the items of the purchase request)
 - "Look up the Inventory List": on, each line picks an item of the Inventory Center
   (inventory and assembly items, with the quantity on hand); its description, default
   unit and the price of the barangay as a customer (its price level) fill in, and the unit
   and price stay editable. Off, the lines are typed freely. The switch is kept.
 - PR est. amount = quantity x PR estimated unit cost, worked out at once, with its total.
 - Add 1 line, 5 lines or 10 lines; the lines are sent as one field, so a purchase request
   of hundreds of lines is saved (no "max_input_vars" limit).
The disbursement voucher (Forms & Proformas > a DV > Edit > Fill in the blanks)
 - Gross amount, VAT and EWT. The taxes, the check amount (gross less VAT and EWT) and the
   amount in words change on the page as you type, and are worked out again on saving:
     VAT 5% = gross / 1.12 x 5%          VAT 3% = gross x 3%
     EWT 2% = gross / 1.12 x 2%          EWT 1% = gross x 1%
   EWT 1% beside VAT 5% is gross / 1.12 x 1%, as on the company's DV:
     29,750.00: VAT 5% 1,328.13 + EWT 1% 265.63 = 1,593.76; check amount PhP28,156.24.
 - The amounts are kept with the transaction ("Fill again from the records" uses them) and
   the Cheque ready dialog expects that check amount. A DV prepared before version 34 gets
   the boxes after "Fill again from the records".
Cheque ready: payment released (Process tab)
 - Tradename (payee of the cheque): every active tradename, the transaction's own chosen
   first. It is kept with the cheque and shown on the Process tab and the timeline.
Request for quotation and IAR (Proforma & Form Templates)
 - The request for quotation in the barangay's format: the BAC letterhead (seal left,
   Picture A right, REPUBLIC OF THE PHILIPPINES ... BIDS AND AWARD COMMITTEE), DATE and
   QUOTATION NO., the request with the barangay's e-mail and hall, the BAC Chairman, the
   General Conditions 1 to 6, UNIT | ITEM DESCRIPTION | QUANTITY | UNIT PRICE | TOTAL with
   ***nothing follows*** and the TOTAL line, Printed Name / Signature, NAME OF ESTABLISHMENT
   and DATE. Its letterhead, footer, pictures and text are changed on the template as before.
 - The IAR: RIS No. and Date beside the IAR No. and Date; the lines Requisitioning
   Office/Dept. and Responsibility Center Code are gone.
Contracting Settings
 - The SOP card: the rates of the withholding tax, profit and receipt deduction drop-downs.
Every page
 - Every page and saved record (329 in all) was opened on a 390 px phone, a 360 px phone
   and a computer: nothing runs past the screen. The new boxes were laid out for phones.
The User's Manual has the new sections (Help on each page).
