G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 31 (2026-11-18)
=========================================================================================

Fix: New company stopped at "Step verify: The new database does not match the program:
2026_11_03_barangay_sop_forms.sql, 2026_11_10_letterheads_bid_forms_types.sql", even with
Company 1 fully up to date.

The cause
---------
Your database was not the problem: running those two scripts again could not help.
A new company receives a copy of the set-up lists of Company 1. The steps and papers of the SOP
(Barangay Contracts) were missing from that list, so the new database got the SOP tables empty.
The final check of the set-up looks for the first SOP step and papers to recognise those two
upgrades, did not find them in the new database, and stopped.

The fix
-------
app/Provision.php          the SOP steps and the SOP papers are now copied to a new company with
                           the other Barangay Contracts lists. Who made or approved a copied list
                           entry (form templates, requirement library, references, SOP steps) is
                           left blank on the copy, since those people are users of Company 1.
views/manual/chapters/13-company-owner.html, 14-super-admin.html, views/manual/search.json, README.md
                           the list of what a new company receives now names the Barangay Contracts lists

No database change. Copy the files over the program folder, keeping the folders.

The company whose set-up failed
-------------------------------
Log in as the Super Admin, open Companies > JJM Trading and Services, type the owner's username,
full name, e-mail and temporary password again (they are never kept), and press Set it up again.
The company then becomes active with its own database (government_contracting_system_company_002),
its Company Code and its owner, who changes the temporary password at the first log-in.
