The Barangay Contracts module runs the company's work with barangays and other procuring entities from start to end: the opportunity, the eligibility papers, the costing and the bid, its approval and submission, the award, the deliveries and the entity's acceptance, change orders, billing packs, collections with the taxes the entity withholds, retention, and the closeout. It keeps every paper in a document vault, prepares the letters, proposals, contracts and receipts from reusable proformas, and posts everything that touches the books through the accounting modules, so inventories, receivables, payables, sales and taxes stay in one ledger.

In the sidebar the module has four groups:

  • Transactions: Contracting Dashboard, Projects & Contracts, Approval Queue, Deliveries & Acceptance, Billing Packs, Collections & Retention, Forms & Proformas, Document Vault.
  • Lists: Barangays & Procuring Entities.
  • Setup: Proforma & Form Templates, Compliance Requirement Library, Procurement Modes, Regulatory References, Contracting Settings, SOP Steps & Papers.
  • Reports, under the heading Barangay Contract Reports: nineteen reports, listed at the end of this chapter.
Important

The module follows the procurement rules; it does not interpret them. Amount ceilings, thresholds, securities, documentary requirements and prescribed forms change, and the procuring entity's own bidding documents govern each procurement. The program never assumes a ceiling or a rate: they are left blank until someone in the company enters them after checking the current law, its implementing rules and the GPPB issuances (see Regulatory References). Republic Act No. 12009 (the New Government Procurement Act) governs procurements started after it took effect; RA 9184 still governs those started before it.

One record, the project, follows a job from the first notice to the closeout. Its phase is shown on every page of the project as a track of twelve steps:

Besides its phase, a project has a status: Open (an opportunity or a bid), Awarded (a contract in force), Lost, No bid, Cancelled (by the entity) or Closed.

Maker, checker, approver

Four kinds of records are controlled: the bid, billing packs, change orders and prepared forms. Each goes Draft > For review > Reviewed > Approved, and can be Returned for correction (with the reason, which the preparer sees) or Reopened by an approver. Nobody reviews or approves what they sent for review, and the approver is not the checker (Contracting Settings > Segregation of duties). A project can name its own checker and approver; then only they, or the alternate they named while away, take those steps. An approver's role carries an approval limit (System Administration > Roles > special permission Max contract / billing amount this role may approve (Barangay Contracts); blank means no limit).

The entity's own acts

The notice of award, the purchase order or contract, the notice to proceed, the inspection and acceptance, an approved variation order and the payment are acts of the procuring entity. The program records them from the entity's own papers, with their dates and a copy in the vault, and never presents an internal approval as the entity's decision: an award needs the notice, an acceptance needs the inspection report, a change order changes the contract only with the entity's approval on file.

The company's SOP

The company's Standard Operating Procedure for barangay transactions runs inside the program. A transaction on the SOP follows one of two processes, step by step, in the order of the SOP; each step says what the SOP asks, lists its papers and checks, and is marked done only when nothing is missing. The company prepares the barangay's own papers (purchase request, purchase order, IAR, RIS, canvass, abstract, disbursement voucher, notices); the barangay checks and signs them, and the signed copies (PDF, JPG or JPEG scans or photos) are attached for tracing and downloading.

ProcessWhen
Small value procurement (SOP)Transactions below the small value limit of Contracting Settings (the SOP's "below 100K"): nine steps, from the transaction form to the release of the papers
Bidding (SOP)Fifteen steps of the bidding process, then the six steps of the payment process flow
Twelve phases (without the SOP)The phases and the compliance checklist described above, as before

The process is chosen when the transaction is recorded (the amount suggests one) and can change until a step is done. The SOP keeps the books the same way as any contract: the award, the delivery and acceptance, the billing pack and its invoice, and the payment received with the taxes withheld are the module's usual records, reached from the steps.

Papers and signed copies

Each paper of a step shows where it stands: To prepare, Prepared (a form was made from its proforma), Signed copy attached, On file (a company paper valid in the vault), Held (payment not released) or Not needed.

  • Prepare makes the form from its proforma, filled from the transaction; Print prints it with the format of its template; the form number opens it on Forms & Proformas to correct it.
  • Attach signed files the signed copy (PDF, JPG or JPEG) in the Document Vault under the project; Replace keeps the earlier copy as an older version. Signed copy opens it.
  • Mark signed (bidding, step 10) records that the proprietor signed a paper before it is scanned.
  • Not needed (approvers, with the reason) takes a paper off the transaction; Add another paper to this step adds one the SOP does not list.
  • All papers lists every paper of the transaction with its form and signed copy; Download signed copies gives them all, with the signed canvass forms, as one ZIP file named by step.

Step by step: work a step

  1. Open the transaction: it opens on the Process tab, at the current step (the steps on the left jump to any step).
  2. Read what the SOP says; prepare, print and attach the papers; enter what the step asks (the PhilGEPS posting, the cheque, the forwarding, the release ...).
  3. Tick the checks. Still to do lists what stands in the way.
  4. Click Step N done (with a remark if useful). The next step opens. Each step done goes to the timeline and the audit trail with who did it and when.
Note

The steps are done in order. An approver may Skip a step that does not apply, or Open this step again, with the reason. The steps, their papers and checks are kept on SOP Steps & Papers: a step or paper added there reaches the open transactions.

Example

Barangay San Isidro accepts laptops, monitors, keyboards and mice worth ₱234,000.00 (prices include VAT). The invoice posts Dr Accounts Receivable ₱234,000.00, Cr Sales ₱208,928.57 and Cr Output VAT ₱25,071.43, with the cost of the goods out of the "Delivered, awaiting acceptance" site. The barangay pays by check and withholds 1% income tax on the amount net of VAT (₱2,089.29) and 5% VAT (₱10,446.43): the collection posts Dr Undeposited Funds ₱221,464.28, Dr Creditable Withholding Tax ₱2,089.29, Dr Creditable VAT Withheld ₱10,446.43, Cr Accounts Receivable ₱234,000.00. The taxes appear on the BIR Reports and the Form 2307 tracker.

The accounts are the module default accounts (Module Default Accounts > Barangay Contracts): Contract Revenue - Goods, - Services, - Works, Retention Receivable, Bid and Performance Security Deposits, Liquidated Damages and Loss on Forfeited Security. The withholding taxes come from the Withholding Tax Table; the VAT withheld appears on the Creditable VAT Withheld Schedule.

Setting up

  1. Contracting Settings: the company's registration number, PhilGEPS number and membership, authorized representative, bank details for payment, VAT status, and the controls.
  2. Financial > Module Default Accounts: check the seven Barangay Contracts accounts.
  3. Procurement Modes and Compliance Requirement Library: enter the ceilings and requirements you verified; approve the requirements.
  4. Document Vault > Company papers: file the permits, registrations, tax clearance, PhilGEPS certificate and financial statements once, with their validity.
  5. Barangays & Procuring Entities: add each entity with its officials (Punong Barangay, BAC chair, treasurer).
  6. Tradenames: add every name the company does business under and mark the default one.
  7. Proforma & Form Templates: adapt the letters, quotations, proposals and contracts to the company's wording.
  8. System Administration > Users: give the contracting staff their roles.
  9. People Accountable Set-up: tick the roles allowed as sales representative, preparer, checker, approver, authorized submitter, project manager and collector.

Contracting Dashboard

Where every contract stands. The figures at the top count the opportunities in preparation (and the bids due within seven days), the bids waiting for a result, the value of the contracts in force, what is still to be accepted, what was accepted and not billed, the receivable from the ledger with the retention held, the win rate of the last twelve months and, for those who see costs, the margin on live contracts.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Contracting Dashboard.

  • Needs attention: papers expired or expiring, bids due, replies due to the BAC, mandatory papers not in order, approvals waiting for you, records returned to you, late deliveries, deliveries not yet accepted, goods rejected and not replaced, billings not submitted or not acknowledged, overdue receivables, retention that can be claimed, securities lapsing, overdue tasks and any contract whose figures do not agree with the ledger. Each line opens the record. The same alerts can be e-mailed every morning (see Contracting Settings).
  • Pipeline by phase: the open projects and contracts in each phase, with their amounts; a click lists them.
  • Your approvals, Next 30 days (deadlines, bid openings, due dates, replies, tasks), Procuring entities and Contracts in force with their progress.

Projects & Contracts

The list of projects and, for each, the record of the whole job. The list shows the open and awarded projects by default, with the next date (bid due or delivery due, in red when passed); search by number, title, RFQ, contract or entity, and filter by entity, status or phase. Export gives the list as a CSV file.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Projects & Contracts. Creating needs the create permission, changing the edit permission.

The head of a project shows its title, entity, category, mode, reference, status and the phase track. Under it, the next step box says what the project waits for and holds the buttons for it (Go / No bid, Send for review, Record the submission, Record the award, Start fulfilment, New delivery, New billing pack, Record collection, Close out, Reopen). The tabs hold everything else.

A transaction on the SOP opens on its Process tab, with the steps of the SOP in the head instead of the phases; its phase is still shown under them. The list shows the process and the step each transaction is at, and filters by process; search also finds the transaction form number and the cheque number. On a transaction of the SOP the price schedule has one more column, PR est. cost: the estimated unit cost the purchase request shows (blank = the unit price).

Step by step: record an opportunity

  1. Click New transaction and choose the Process: small value procurement, bidding (both on the SOP) or the twelve phases. For the SOP, fill in the Transaction form (date, the Tradename chosen from the Tradenames list, type, requestor, inspector, the Sales representative, fund, the estimates and targets and the purpose; the series number is given on saving). The numbers and dates of the barangay's papers (PR, PO, RIS, IAR, DV) are filled in on each paper (Fill in the blanks). Type the title as in the notice, choose the Procuring entity, the Category (goods, general services, infrastructure works, consulting) and the Procurement mode; the procurement law follows the mode (RA 9184 only for procurements started under the old law).
  2. Enter the RFQ / ITB number, the PhilGEPS reference, the link to the posting, the ABC, the source of funds, the end-user, the delivery site and the delivery period in calendar days.
  3. Enter the key dates: site inspection, pre-bid conference, last day for questions, the bid deadline and the bid opening.
  4. Enter the terms the RFQ states: retention %, liquidated damages % per day, warranty, payment terms.
  5. Name the people: preparer, checker, approver (three different people), the authorized submitter (the only one who records the bid submission), the project manager and the collector. Each list shows only the active users in the roles set for it on People Accountable Set-up: the users of the roles allowed, or the users named (every active user while nothing is set).
  6. Click Record opportunity. The compliance checklist is made at once from the requirement library, and the company papers already in the vault are linked to it.
  7. Decide Go or No bid with the reason. No bid closes the opportunity (an approver can reopen it).

Positions entered on older transactions stay in the database. The Sales representative list shows only the users in the roles set on People Accountable Set-up (the positions acting as sales representative).

Eligibility and bid papers have to be valid on the bid deadline (then on the day the bid went in); contract, delivery and billing papers while the contract runs. File uploads the paper or picks one already in the vault; a company paper can be filed as such, to be reused on every bid. The Price schedule tab

The bill of quantities: for each line its kind (goods, service, works), an item (optional; it brings the description, unit, cost and tax code), the description, unit, quantity, unit cost and markup (seen only with the View costs and margins permission), the unit price and the tax code. A blank price is the cost plus the markup. Prices include VAT unless Contracting Settings say otherwise. The meter under the lines compares the total with the ABC and turns red above it. Export (CSV for Excel) gives the schedule as a file Excel opens.

Look up the Inventory List (the switch over the lines, since version 34): on, each line has an Item list of the Inventory Center (inventory and assembly items, with the quantity on hand); choosing an item fills its description, its default unit and the price of the barangay as a customer (its price level; the sales price while the barangay is not a customer yet), and both the unit and the price can still be changed. Off, the item column is hidden and the lines are typed freely. The switch is kept with the transaction.

On a transaction of the SOP the lines are the items of the purchase request: PR est. cost is the estimated unit cost (blank = the unit price) and PR est. amount is worked out at once, the quantity times the estimated unit cost, with its total under the lines. Add 1 line, 5 lines or 10 lines adds empty lines at once; the count beside them shows how many are filled in. Empty lines are left out on saving, and a schedule of hundreds of lines is saved in one go.

The schedule can be changed only while the bid is a draft or returned. Each time the bid goes for review a read-only copy of the costing is kept (costing versions). After the award the schedule becomes the contract lines, and only change orders alter them; the tab then shows, per line, the quantities delivered, accepted, rejected and billed.

Step by step: send the bid for approval and submit it

  1. When the checklist and the schedule are ready, click Send for review (next step box). The program refuses while something is not settled and lists what: the go decision, an empty schedule, a line without quantity or price, a bid above the ABC, the deadline passed, a mandatory eligibility or bid paper missing, expired or not reviewed.
  2. The checker opens the bid (Approval Queue or the project) and clicks Mark reviewed, or Return for correction with the reason.
  3. The approver clicks Approve (within their approval limit, before the deadline). The schedule is now read-only.
  4. The authorized submitter submits the bid as the entity asks (by hand, e-mail, PhilGEPS, courier) and records it on the Submissions & letters tab: what, how, the actual date and time, to whom, the tracking number, who received it, and the proof (receiving copy, sent e-mail, courier receipt). A hand-carried or couriered bid needs its proof or at least who received it; a submission after the deadline is refused. The bid is then fixed and the project moves to "Bid submitted".
Note

The program never sends anything to PhilGEPS or to the entity by itself; it records what was actually done, with its proof.

The Submissions & letters tab

All submissions (bid, compliance papers, post-qualification documents, billing packs, follow-ups) with their receipt, and the correspondence: bulletins, clarification requests, notices, meetings and letters, in or out. A letter with a reply due date stays open (and on the dashboard) until it is marked Answered. Recording a bid opening, bulletin, clarification or post-qualification moves a submitted bid to "Evaluation and post-qualification".

Step by step: record the award and start the contract

  1. When the entity's notice of award (or purchase order) comes, open the Award & contract tab and enter its number and date, the date the company received it, the amount awarded (it has to agree with the approved schedule), the PO or contract number, the signing and notice-to-proceed dates, and attach the paper. The due date follows from the start and the delivery period unless typed.
  2. Click Record the award. The entity becomes a customer (type Government) if it is not one yet, a Customer:Job and a class are opened for the contract, and nothing is posted.
  3. Record the securities (bid, performance, warranty) on the same tab. Cash, cashier's and manager's checks and bank drafts post a journal entry to Bid and Performance Security Deposits; a returned security posts back to the bank, a forfeited one to Loss on Forfeited Security.
  4. Click Start fulfilment once the contract is signed or the PO received (works also need the notice to proceed).

If the bid was not awarded, click Not awarded with the reason, the winning bidder and amount and your rank (they feed the Won and Lost report), or Cancelled by the entity. An award the entity cancels before any work is recorded with Award cancelled by the entity.

The Change orders tab

A change order adds to or takes from contract lines (an existing line keeps its price), adds new lines, or extends the time. It goes through maker, checker and approver, and then changes the contract only when Apply to the contract is clicked with the entity's approval (variation order, amended PO, extension letter): its number, date and the paper. The contract amount, the lines and the due date follow.

The Costs, Tasks, Timeline and Ledger tabs

  • Costs (View costs and margins permission): revenue and costs of the contract from the ledger, the margin and the estimated cost to complete; the purchases on the job; Purchase order for this contract makes a purchase order whose lines carry the contract's job and class (received and billed in Vendors as usual).
  • Tasks: tasks, milestones, progress logs, risks and defects with who, when and how far.
  • Timeline: everything that happened, newest first: steps, submissions, letters, deliveries, billings, collections, documents and change orders, with who did them.
  • Ledger: every entry the contract put in the books, by record and event, and whether the contract agrees with the ledger.

Step by step: close out

  1. When everything is delivered, accepted, billed and collected, an approver clicks Close out.
  2. The program checks: nothing accepted and not billed, no delivery waiting for inspection, no billing in preparation, no receivable left on the job, and the contract agrees with the ledger. These cannot be overridden.
  3. Open points (quantities never accepted, retention still held, a security still active, a change order never applied) can be accepted with a reason: tick Close anyway and type it.
  4. A closed, lost, cancelled or no-bid project can be Reopened by an approver, with the reason.

Approval Queue

Everything that waits for the user's review or approval: bids, billing packs, change orders and forms, each with its amount, who sent it and when, and the buttons Mark reviewed, Approve and Return for correction. A bid shows its ABC, margin and checklist; a billing pack its expected deductions and what its pack still lacks. The menu shows the number waiting next to the page name.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Approval Queue. Reviewing needs the edit permission on this page, approving the approve permission.

  • Returned to me: what the user sent that came back, with the reason.
  • Everything in approval: all records being reviewed or approved, whoever they wait for.
  • Alternates: a checker or approver names who acts for them while away (checker, approver or both, from and to). The alternate's steps are recorded as done for the person away, within that person's approval limit, and still not on what the alternate prepared.
  • Decisions: the steps taken since a date (also the Approval Log report).

Deliveries & Acceptance

Goods delivered, services rendered and works accomplished, and the entity's inspection and acceptance of each.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Deliveries & Acceptance, or New delivery on a contract.

Step by step: a delivery and its acceptance

  1. Click New delivery and choose the contract. Enter the date, the kind, the site the goods leave from, the vehicle and who received them at the entity.
  2. Type the quantity delivered on each line (Everything left fills what is left of the contract), with serial or batch numbers. A quantity beyond what is left of the contract is refused: more needs an approved change order.
  3. Click Record delivery. Stocked items move to the site "Delivered, awaiting acceptance" (an inventory transfer); they are sold only when billed.
  4. Print delivery receipt (or the accomplishment report for services and works) prints it for signature; Prepare as a form makes an editable copy.
  5. When the entity's inspection and acceptance report comes, enter on the same page the dates, the report number, the inspectors and, per line, the quantities accepted and rejected (All accepted fills them), and attach the signed report. Rejected stocked goods move back to the warehouse they left from.

Only accepted quantities can be billed. Acceptance can be corrected later, but never below what was already billed. A delivery not yet billed can be voided (its stock movements are reversed).

Billing Packs

A billing pack bills accepted quantities: the lines, the invoice, the papers the entity asks for, the submission and the entity's acknowledgment.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Billing Packs, or New billing pack on a contract.

Step by step: bill, invoice, submit

  1. Click New billing pack, choose the contract, the date, the kind (partial, progress, milestone, final), the period and the BIR-registered sales invoice number. Type the quantity to bill on each line (Everything accepted fills them). More than was accepted and not billed is refused, and so are billings that together would exceed the contract.
  2. Click Prepare billing. Each line is split into its amount and VAT, and the expected deductions are worked out: income tax and VAT withheld (from the Withholding Tax Table, on the amount net of VAT), retention and liquidated damages (for deliveries after the due date, at the rate of the contract; it can be typed over).
  3. Send for review; the checker reviews and the approver approves, as for the bid.
  4. The billing officer clicks Issue the invoice: one invoice is posted to the contract's Customer:Job. A second invoice for the same billing is refused, and so is a sales invoice number already used.
  5. Complete the pack: each paper the settings ask for shows whether it is on file (a scan, a final form, the signed delivery receipts and inspection reports of the contract). File it uploads a missing one; Prepare the form makes the statement of account, transmittal or billing letter.
  6. Record the submission (how, when, to whom, proof); a pack with a required paper missing is refused when Contracting Settings say so.
  7. Record the entity's acknowledgment (when, by whom, the logbook number, the receiving copy).

A billing without collections can be voided: its invoice is voided in the books and its quantities can be billed again.

Collections & Retention

Payments of the entities against billings, retention released, returned checks and the BIR Forms 2307 received.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Collections & Retention, or Record collection on a billing or a contract.

Step by step: a payment

  1. Choose the billing paid. The page shows what is open on the invoice, any returned check still to be covered, and the expected taxes and net cash.
  2. Fill as expected (paid in full) fills the taxes, retention and liquidated damages as expected and the cash as the rest. Then change the amounts to what the check or LDDAP-ADA and the entity's 2307 actually show.
  3. Enter how it was paid (check with its number and date, LDDAP-ADA with its number, transfer, cash), the drawee bank, where it was received (Undeposited Funds for a check to be deposited, or the bank), the receipt issued.
  4. Enter what the entity deducted: income tax withheld, VAT withheld, retention, liquidated damages, other deductions (with what they are and the account), bank charges. Settled on the billing adds them up.
  5. Tick BIR Form 2307 received and attach it if it came with the payment (it can be filed later on the collection).
  6. Click Post the collection. A difference between the taxes typed and the Withholding Tax Table needs an explanation.

Check returned: on the collection, enter the date, the bank's reason and any bank fee. A check not yet deposited voids the payment; a deposited one is charged back to the receivable. The billing is open again; record the replacement as a new collection.

Retention released: choose the contract, the amount (up to what is held), how and into which bank. It posts a deposit that clears Retention Receivable.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Forms & Proformas, the Forms tab of a project, the papers of the Process tab, and the Prepare boxes of deliveries, billings and collections.

The amounts of the disbursement voucher

Since version 34 the Fill in the blanks of a disbursement voucher starts with three boxes: Gross amount, VAT and EWT. Type the gross amount and choose the rates; the taxes, the total withheld, the check amount (the gross less the VAT and the EWT) and the amount in words change on the page as you type, and are worked out again when the DV is saved.

RateHow it is computedGross 29,750.00
VAT 5%gross ÷ 1.12 × 5%1,328.13
VAT 3%gross × 3%892.50
EWT 2%gross ÷ 1.12 × 2%531.25
EWT 1%gross × 1%; beside the VAT of 5%, gross ÷ 1.12 × 1%297.50; 265.63 beside VAT 5%

So a gross of Php29,750.00 with VAT 5% and EWT 1% gives 1,328.13 + 265.63 = 1,593.76 withheld and a check amount of PhP28,156.24 (TWENTY-EIGHT THOUSAND ONE HUNDRED FIFTY-SIX PESOS AND 24/100). The gross amount and the rates are kept with the transaction: Fill again from the records uses them, and the Cheque ready dialog expects that check amount. A DV prepared before version 34 gets the three boxes after Fill again from the records.

Step by step: prepare, approve and print a form

  1. Choose the template and, where it applies, the delivery, billing or collection; click Prepare. The form opens as a draft on paper of its size.
  2. Edit the text in the page: the toolbar sets headings, bold, alignment, lists, tables and page breaks. Pasted text comes in clean.
  3. Fill in the blanks lists the fields that had no value in the records (shown highlighted in the page): type each one there, or click it in the page and type.
  4. Set the signatory, the paper (A4, Letter, Folio / long, Legal, A5, half letter) and the orientation; click Save. Fill again from the records makes the form again from the current template and records (what was typed is replaced).
  5. Send for review; the checker reviews and the approver approves. A form with blanks cannot be sent.
  6. Make final and file in the vault: the form is locked, filed as a document (where it answers its checklist item or billing pack paper) and printed without the DRAFT mark. A prescribed form needs the official version it follows.
  7. Print (or Print draft, with DRAFT across every page) and Word (a .doc file that opens in Microsoft Word or LibreOffice).
Important

The omnibus sworn statement, the bid securing declaration and the bid form are prescribed forms: their wording is issued by the GPPB or the procuring entity. Their templates are shells: paste the current official text from the entity's bidding documents, fill the blanks, and state the official version followed. Do not rewrite a prescribed form.

Document Vault

Every paper of the contracting work. Each file keeps its type, number, issuer, dates of issue and validity, a checksum taken when it was filed (a file changed on disk is refused when opened), its versions, its review and whether it is final (locked). Confidential papers (financial bids, costing) open only for users with the View costs and margins permission. Opening and downloading are recorded in the audit trail.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Document Vault. Filing needs the create permission, reviewing the edit permission, voiding the delete permission.

  • Company papers: one card for each kind of company paper (PhilGEPS certificate, mayor's permit, registration, BIR 2303, tax clearance, PCAB license, financial statements, NFCC ...), with its validity: Valid, Expires soon, Expired, For review, Not on file. A company paper is filed once and linked to every bid that asks for it while it is valid.
  • For review: papers filed and waiting for another person's review: Approve, Approve as final (lock) or Return with what to correct. Nobody reviews a paper they filed.
  • Expiring and expired: papers whose validity ends within the alert days of Contracting Settings.
  • All documents: search by title, number, issuer, tag, file name or project.

A renewed permit is filed as a new version of the old one: the checklists that used the old version move to the new one and need its review. Accepted files: PDF, pictures (JPG, PNG, GIF, WEBP), Word, Excel, PowerPoint and Publisher files of every version (below), Rich Text, OpenDocument, CSV and text, up to the size set on Contracting Settings. A file whose content does not match its extension (a zip renamed .docx, a text renamed .ppt), or that looks like a program or a script, is refused; a virus scanner can be set by the administrator.

Tradenames

The names the company does business under. Each transaction picks one on its transaction form, and the transaction form, the canvass of the company and the other papers print the tradename of that transaction. The default tradename fills a new transaction.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > Tradenames, the Edit the list link above the Tradename box of the transaction form, or the Tradenames link on Contracting Settings.

Step by step

  1. Add: type the tradename (and a description), tick Default if new transactions should start with it, and click Add.

SOP Steps & Papers

Compliance Requirement Library

What the procuring entities ask for, with when each requirement applies: procurement law, category, procurement modes, ABC band, effective dates, and the entity (for a requirement of one entity only). Each requirement says its stage, whether it is mandatory, conditional or optional, whether it has a validity date, the document type that answers it, the form that prepares it, and its legal basis with a link to the official source.

Procurement Modes

The modes of procurement under RA 12009 (competitive bidding, limited source bidding, direct contracting, direct acquisition, repeat order, small value procurement, negotiated procurement, direct sales ...) and, for procurements started before it, those of RA 9184. For each: its legal basis and link, the ABC ceiling, whether bid and performance securities are asked, the usual retention and the effective dates. The ceiling is blank until someone enters the figure verified against the current rules; a project with an ABC above a ceiling that was entered is refused.

People Accountable Set-up

Who may be chosen for each person of a transaction: the sales representative (the positions acting as sales representative) and the people accountable for the project: preparer (maker), checker / reviewer, approver, authorized submitter, project manager and collector. Since version 34 each function is set one of two ways:

  • Roles: tick the user roles allowed; the drop-downs list the active users who have one of them (a new user given the role is listed at once).
  • Users: tick the people allowed by name; the drop-downs list only them (while they are active).

A function with nothing ticked lists every active user.

Where to find it: Barangay Contracts > People Accountable Set-up, or the link under the People accountable for the project card of the transaction form.

FunctionWhere it is chosenWhat the person does
Sales representativeTransaction formGives the transaction details, receives the tasks of Request / prepare details, and delivers the papers when they are released by the sales representative.
Preparer (maker)People accountable for the projectPrepares the bid and the papers. A new transaction starts with the person recording it, when that person is in the roles allowed.
Checker / reviewerPeople accountable for the projectReviews what the preparer made. Left blank: anyone with the review right.
ApproverPeople accountable for the projectApproves the bid, billing packs, change orders and forms. Left blank: anyone with the approve right.
Authorized submitterPeople accountable for the project, and the SOP step of the authorized representativeThe only person who records the bid submission.
Project managerPeople accountable for the projectRuns the delivery or the works after the award.
CollectorPeople accountable for the projectFollows up and receives the payments.

Step by step

  1. Open Barangay Contracts > People Accountable Set-up.
  2. For each function, choose Roles or Users.
  3. With Roles, open the Roles allowed list and tick the roles (for example Sales Representative for the sales representative, Contracts - Project Manager for the project manager). With Users, open the Users allowed list and tick the people (type a few letters of a name to find it). Clear leaves the function open to every active user.
  4. Check Listed now: the users the drop-down will offer. "Nobody yet" means none of the roles ticked has an active user (give a user one of them on System Administration > User Accounts), or the users ticked are inactive.
  5. Click Save.
Note

The roles are those of User Role Configuration; their names stand for the positions of the staff (Sales Representative, Contracts - Project Manager, ...). A person already chosen on a saved transaction stays on it and is still shown, even when their role is no longer allowed; choosing anyone else is refused ("... cannot be the project manager: People Accountable Set-up allows only the roles ... / only ... for it"). A function is set one way at a time: saving it with Users clears its roles, and with Roles clears its users. The preparer, checker and approver still have to be three different people when that control is on. The upgrade to version 33 ticks the roles named like Sales Representative for the sales representative and leaves the other functions open; the upgrade to version 34 keeps every function as it was (by its roles). Changes are recorded in the audit trail. A new company made with New company copies the roles of each function, not the users named.

Barangay Contract Reports

Each report has the filters, column choices, printing, export and memorizing of every report of the program (see Working with Reports).

Bid Register

Every opportunity by bid deadline: entity, mode, category, RFQ, ABC, bid and its percentage of the ABC, deadline, submission, bid status, status and phase. Filters: dates, entity, status.

Won and Lost Opportunities

The bids submitted in the dates with their result, rank, winning bidder and amount, how far above it the company was, and the reason; the win rate by count and by amount, and what was won by category.

Eligibility and Compliance Matrix

The checklist of every open project (or contract in force) with each requirement's document, validity and status, and the state of every company paper.

Tender and Document Submission Log

Every submission in the dates: what, how, to whom, tracking number, who received it and when, acknowledgment, whether the proof is on file, and whether a bid was on time.

Contract Status: Awarded, Delivered, Billed, Collected

Per contract: amount (with change orders), accepted and its percentage, billed, settled, cash collected, taxes withheld, retention held and receivable from the ledger.

Delivery and Implementation Variance

Per contract line: contract quantity, delivered, rejected, accepted, still to be accepted, value left and, for contracts past their due date, the liquidated damages exposure.

Inspection and Acceptance Register

Every delivery with its acceptance date, the days the entity took, the report number, the inspectors, the value accepted and the quantity rejected.

Billing Pack Completeness

Every billing with how many of its required papers are on file and which are missing, when it was submitted and acknowledged, and how long it has waited.

A/R Aging by Barangay

The open invoices of the contracts on a date, by entity, in aging columns (current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, over 90 days past due).

Collection Performance by Barangay

Per entity: billed, settled and the rate, cash, taxes withheld, number of payments, average days from billing and from submission to payment, returned checks and what is open now.

Change Order Register

Change orders with their amount, percentage of the original contract, days added, status, the entity's approval and when they were applied.

Retention and Warranty Tracker

Per contract: retention deducted, released and held (ledger), completion, warranty end, when the retention can be claimed, and the performance and warranty securities.

Project Profitability

Per contract: amount, estimated cost and margin, revenue and costs from the ledger, margin and its percentage, estimated cost to complete (View costs and margins permission).

Taxes Withheld by Barangays (2307 Tracker)

Collections with taxes withheld: withholding agent and TIN, quarter, amount settled, income tax and VAT withheld, any difference from the table, and whether the Form 2307 was received. Use it to collect the missing certificates before the quarterly and annual returns.

Document Expiry and Missing Papers

Papers expired or expiring within the days chosen, company papers not on file, and mandatory papers missing on open projects and contracts.

Cash Collection Forecast

The net cash expected by month: open invoices (dated by each entity's usual delay between billing and payment), what is still to be delivered and billed, and retention after the warranty. An estimate, not a promise.

Contracts-to-Ledger Reconciliation

Per contract, what the module says against the ledger: billed against invoiced, settled against applied, retention against the Retention Receivable on the job, and anything posted on the job outside the module. A contract that does not agree is shown in red with the difference; it usually means an invoice, payment or credit memo was voided or changed in the accounting pages.

Approval Log

Every maker, checker and approver step in the dates, by user and record, with the alternate who acted for someone away, the amount and the remarks.

Transaction Monitoring (SOP)

The monitoring of the SOP: each transaction from its transaction form to the signed transmittal, with its series number, date, barangay, purpose, type, process, where it stands, gross amount, taxes withheld, cheque number and amount, payment (waiting or released), SI and OR numbers, 2307, signed copies against papers, when it went to accounting, when and how it was released, and whether the signed transmittal came back. Filters: dates (of the transaction form), entity, process, where it stands (not finished, waiting for payment, transmittal not returned, closed).
