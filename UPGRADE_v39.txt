G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 39 (2026-11-26)
=========================================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_GOVERNMENT_CONTRUCTING_SYSTEM_v7.xlsx:
 1. A user may hold up to two roles on a transaction; three roles and above are not
    allowed.
 2. The price schedule moves to step 4: "Preparing Billing Documents" becomes
    "Preparing Price Schedule".
 3. The purchase request is saved with no P.R. No. and Date.
 4. Multiple rows on the purchase request (it may run to 2 to 5 pages).
 5. Every upload is made smaller before it is kept: 2 to 5 MB under 1 MB, 6 MB and above
    under 2 MB.
 6. Step 4: the SI, OR and 2307 are removed (not needed in actual practice).
 7. The DV is saved with no DV No., Date and Fund.
 8. The IAR no. and date, the RIS no. and date and the invoice no. and date may be blank.
 9. The customer form gets the BAC Chairman, the BAC members (2 or more) and the Barangay
    Chairman; they fill the forms; completed contracts keep the officers they had, current
    and new forms take the new ones.
10. A new menu: Barangay Project Monitoring Board, filtered by Small Value Procurement /
    Bidding / Twelve phases, Barangay, Amount, Supplier, Cheque Amount, CR, DR, Sales
    Invoice / Service Invoice and Status, with the columns of the monitoring sheet.
11. A transaction is saved with the PhilGEPS reference, source link, source of funds and
    ABC empty.

This package holds ONLY the files that are new or changed since version 38, in the same
folders. Copy them over your installation (folder government-contracting-system); every
other file stays as it is.

Upgrading an installation of version 38
---------------------------------------
1. Back up the database and the program folder (storage/ and config/config.php too).
2. Copy the files of this package over your installation. config/config.php is not in the
   package; yours stays.
3. Run the new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
   Navicat: Execute SQL File):

       database/migrations/2026_11_26_roles_price_schedule_officers_board.sql

   It needs 2026_11_25_cities_form_values_signatories.sql (version 38) to have been run
   first. With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin:
   Companies > Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program
   shows a page naming it. It is written for MySQL 5.5 and is safe to run a second time.
4. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (the scripts and styles changed, and a new script
   makes the uploads smaller).
5. Customer Center: open each barangay > Edit and check its Barangay officers (Barangay
   Chairman, BAC Chairman, BAC members). A barangay already on Barangays & Procuring
   Entities shows the officers it has there.
6. A small value procurement that version 38 already awarded at the canvass and that is
   at step 4 now shows step 4 as "Prepare price schedule" with its award: check that the
   price schedule agrees with the company's quotation and mark the step done.
7. Segregation of duties (Contracting Settings) now allows two roles per person. A saved
   transaction where one person holds three roles keeps them until it is next saved; it
   is then refused until one role is given to someone else.

What the migration does
-----------------------
 - One column on brgy_templates: optional_fields (after required_fields), the fields of a
   template that may stay blank. Set for: Purchase request (pr.no, pr.date); Disbursement
   voucher (dv.no, dv.date, dv.fund); Inspection and acceptance report (iar.no, iar.date,
   ris.no, ris.date, iar.invoice_no, iar.invoice_date); Requisition and issue slip
   (ris.no, ris.date). The P.R., IAR and RIS numbers are no longer required fields of
   those templates.
 - Step 4 of the small value procurement becomes "Prepare price schedule" (section
   "Price schedule"), with no papers and no checks of its own. On the transactions that
   have not done step 4 yet, the SI, OR and BIR Form 2307 with nothing attached are taken
   off the step; a paper with a signed copy or a form stays. A step 4 you had already
   changed on SOP Steps & Papers (another action) is left as it is.
 - The page Barangay Contracts > Barangay Project Monitoring Board (menu Transactions,
   after Projects & Contracts), given to every role that opens Projects & Contracts, with
   view and its print right (Export, Print).
 - One line in the audit trail saying the migration was run.
Nothing else is deleted, no amount changes and nothing is posted to the books. The
bidding process is not touched (its SI, OR and 2307 and special case F stay).

What is new
-----------
1. Two roles at most per person (Barangay Contracts > Projects & Contracts)
 - Of the sales representative, preparer, checker, approver, authorized submitter,
   project manager and collector, one person may be named for up to two. Choosing a
   third shows a warning on the form at once; saving is refused: "Maria Santos would
   hold three roles on this transaction (sales representative, preparer and checker). A
   person may hold up to two roles: choose another person for one of them." The SOP step
   of the authorized representative is checked the same way.
 - On a bid, billing pack, change order or form, the preparer may also review it; then
   another person approves ("You prepared and reviewed this record. A person may hold up
   to two of its roles (preparer, checker, approver), so another person approves it.").
   The person who filed a paper in the Document Vault, or proposed a requirement, may
   review or approve it.
 - Switched off with Segregation of duties on Contracting Settings, as before.

2. and 6. Small value procurement, step 4: Prepare price schedule
 - Step 3 (canvass): a company quotation that is not the lowest that passed records the
   transaction as lost, as before; otherwise the price schedule is next (no award yet).
   The company's quotation is typed while the price schedule is still empty.
 - Step 4 shows the items, the company's quotation (agrees / does not agree) and the
   award. "Take the N items of the purchase request BFM-..." copies the rows typed on the
   purchase request into an empty price schedule (quantity, unit, description, the
   estimated cost as the price). "Make the company's quotation X" sets the quotation in
   the canvass to the schedule's total.
 - Step 4 is done only when the schedule has items with quantities and prices, agrees
   with the company's quotation, and that quotation is still the lowest that passed.
   Done: the purchase order is recorded as the award (its number and date from the PO
   form when typed there), the abstract, PO and transaction form are refreshed, and the
   draft forms whose item table is empty (PR, PO, IAR, RIS, request for quotation) get
   the items.
 - The SI, OR and 2307 are no longer asked for. The payment (Cheque ready, follow-ups,
   then the delivery and acceptance, billing pack and payment received) has its own box,
   Payment, below the steps. Step 5 (Review) no longer asks for a reviewer other than
   the one who prepared billing papers.

3., 7. and 8. Blanks that may stay blank (Forms & Proformas)
 - The P.R. no. and date (purchase request); the DV no., date and fund (disbursement
   voucher); the IAR no. and date, the RIS no. and date and the invoice no. and date
   (IAR); the RIS no. and date (RIS) are listed in Fill in the blanks marked "may stay
   blank". They are not counted as blanks: the form is prepared, sent for review and made
   final without them, and prints an empty space in their place (also in Word).
 - Proforma & Form Templates > Edit: "Fields that may stay blank" (keys separated by
   commas, e.g. pr.no, pr.date) sets them for any template; the template page lists them.

4. The rows of the purchase request
 - The purchase request, purchase order, IAR, RIS and request for quotation list their
   item rows in the Fill in the blanks panel ("Purchase request items"): one row of boxes
   each (description, qty, unit, est. cost ...), Add a row, add below, move up / down,
   remove; the amounts and the total are worked out as you type, and the table in the
   page follows ("***nothing follows***" after the last row). A click on a row in the page
   jumps to its boxes; Undo / Redo cover the rows.
 - Any number of rows: a long purchase request prints on 2 pages or more with the column
   headings repeated on each page and no row split across two pages.

5. Uploads made smaller
 - In the browser, before sending: a PDF or a picture (JPG, PNG, WEBP, BMP) of 2 MB up to
   6 MB is brought under 1 MB, one of 6 MB and above under 2 MB. A picture is drawn again
   smaller (a PNG with see-through parts stays a PNG); each page of a PDF is drawn as a
   picture and the pages put back together as a PDF (pdf.js 4.10.38, Apache 2.0, in
   assets/vendor/pdfjs, so it works offline). The name stays; the note under the box
   says "Made smaller before sending: FS AND ITR.pdf: 8.6 MB to 1.6 MB". Save waits for it.
 - Everywhere files are chosen: the Document Vault, signed copies, the canvass forms,
   attachments of transactions (kept in the database), logos and seals. Not the backup
   restore and the CSV import.
 - On the server: a picture that still arrives at 2 MB or more (a browser that could not
   make it smaller) is made smaller the same way (PHP's GD) before the Document Vault or
   the attachments keep it.
 - Other files (Word, Excel ...) are kept as they are; one larger than the server takes is
   refused at once with a message.

9. Barangay officers on the customer (Customer Center > a barangay > Edit)
 - Barangay Chairman (Punong Barangay), BAC Chairman and BAC members (two boxes to start,
   Add a member for more, the cross to remove). The customer's card lists them.
 - They are kept on Barangays & Procuring Entities with their terms (a customer not
   listed there yet is added). A change starts that day: the officer replaced keeps the
   days served; a correction on the same day is simply corrected.
 - The forms take them: a completed contract (closed, lost, no bid, cancelled) keeps the
   officers of the day it was completed, on new forms too; a contract open or in force
   takes the new names on new forms, and on its drafts when they are opened ("The
   barangay's officers changed since this form was made: Punong Barangay Hon. ... (was
   Hon. ...). Save to keep, or Undo to take them back."), capitals kept where the page
   had them.

10. Barangay Contracts > Barangay Project Monitoring Board
 - Tabs: All processes, Small value procurement, Bidding, Twelve phases.
 - Filters: search (particulars, trans. code, check no.), barangay, supplier (tradename),
   status (ongoing, waiting for the cheque, cheque released, process completed, closed,
   lost / no bid / cancelled), gross amount and cheque amount (from, to), C.R. no.,
   D.R. no., invoice no.
 - Columns: Check date, Trans. code, Barangay, Particulars, Gross amount, Cheque amount,
   Supplier, Tax %, Sales / service invoice, C.R., D.R., Check no., Process completed,
   Status (with the payment under it); totals under the columns and in four figures over
   the board. Export (CSV with the process and payment columns) and Print. Each
   transaction code opens its project. On a phone each project is a card.

11. Blank PhilGEPS reference, source link, source of funds and ABC
 - These four already saved empty. Now step 1 of the small value procurement needs
   neither the ABC nor the items (they come at step 4), and an ABC not entered shows blank
   (not 0.00) on the transaction.

The User's Manual explains all of it (Barangay Contracts: Maker, checker, approver; the

Monitoring Board; Customers > Barangay officers; Getting Started > attachments), with
three new pictures and the Process tab picture taken again.
