G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 38 (2026-11-25)
=========================================================================================

The requests of ADDITIONAL_MODULE_FOR_GOVERNMENT_CONTRUCTING_SYSTEM_v6.xlsx:
 1. Customer Center: add, edit or delete a City/Municipality; delete only one not used.
 2. Forms: the Signatory / Position beside the form and the signature in the page kept
    the same ("Jerome T. Cuizon / Affiant" on the omnibus sworn statement).
 3. Undo and redo on filling the blanks, and on every form to be filled up.
 4. The IAR's [Purchase order no.] and [Purchase order date] filled from the step before.
 5. Fill in the blanks fields for the signatories of the IAR (Inspection Officer, Supply
    and/or Property Custodian) and of every other form with signatories.

This package holds ONLY the files that are new or changed since version 37, in the same
folders. Copy them over your installation (folder government-contracting-system); every
other file stays as it is.

Upgrading an installation of version 37
---------------------------------------
1. Back up the database and the program folder (storage/ and config/config.php too).
2. Copy the files of this package over your installation. config/config.php is not in the
   package; yours stays.
3. Run the new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
   Navicat: Execute SQL File):

       database/migrations/2026_11_25_cities_form_values_signatories.sql

   It needs 2026_11_24_estimates_sworn_statements.sql (version 37) to have been run first.
   With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies >
   Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page
   naming it. It is written for MySQL 5.5 and is safe to run a second time.
4. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser (the scripts and styles changed).
5. Customers > Lists > City/Municipality List: check the list (it starts with the cities
   of your procuring entities), add the others your customers are in, and give the zip
   codes. Then choose the City/Municipality on each customer.
6. Drafts of the disbursement voucher, requisition and issue slip, transaction form and
   the two statements (ongoing and similar contracts) made before version 38: open the
   draft > Edit > "Fill again from the records" to have their signatories listed in the
   Fill in the blanks panel. What was typed on such a draft is replaced, so do it before
   filling it in. Every other form lists its signatories as it is.

What the migration does
-----------------------
 - New table cities (id, name, province, zip_code, is_active, who and when), filled with
   the cities and municipalities of the procuring entities (Barangays & Procuring
   Entities), each once.
 - One empty column on customers: city_id (after zip_code), with a foreign key to cities,
   so the database itself refuses to delete a city a customer uses. The customers made
   for the procuring entities get the entity's city.
 - The page Customers > Lists > City/Municipality List, given to every role that can open
   the Customer Center, with the same rights (Administrator: everything).
 - New table brgy_form_values (transaction, field, value, the form it came from): what is
   filled into the blanks of a transaction's forms, for its next forms.
 - The templates "Transaction form", "Requisition and issue slip (RIS)", "Disbursement
   voucher (DV)", "Statement of all ongoing government and private contracts" and
   "Statement of the single largest completed contract similar to the contract to be bid
   (SLCC)": their signatures are marked so the forms list
   them. Only the markup around the names and positions changes; nothing prints
   differently. Each gets a new version and the one before is kept (Proforma & Form
   Templates > the template > Versions; "Put back" restores it). A template you had
   changed so that its signature lines are no longer the ones supplied is left as it is.
 - One line in the audit trail saying the migration was run.
Nothing is deleted, no amount changes and nothing is posted to the books. Forms already
prepared keep their text.

What is new
-----------
1. Customers > Lists > City/Municipality List (also "Edit the list" on the customer form)
 - The list with Type to find, and for each: City / municipality, Province, Zip code, how
   many customers use it (Customers), Edit and Delete.
 - Add a city or municipality: name (required), province, zip code, Active. The same name
   twice only with different provinces ("Lapu-Lapu City, Cebu is already on the list.").
 - Delete only when no customer uses it: for one in use the bin is greyed out ("Used by 2
   customer(s): it cannot be deleted"), and a delete sent anyway is refused: "Cebu City
   cannot be deleted: 2 customers use it. Choose another city or municipality on those
   customers first, or make it inactive so it is no longer offered."
 - Untick Active to stop offering one; a customer that has it keeps it.
 - Every add, change and delete is in the audit trail; rights per role on User Role
   Configuration (view, create, edit, delete).
 Customer Center
 - The customer form has City/Municipality (after Active, where the Excel file marks it),
   a list you can type in showing "Name, Province". Choosing one with a zip code fills an
   empty Zip code. "Edit the list" opens the list in a new tab; when you come back to the
   form the box shows the list as it is now and keeps what you chose.
 - The customer's card shows the city with its province; the list finds the customers of
   a city by its name (Enter); Export list has City/Municipality and Province; custom
   reports have a City/Municipality column; Import from CSV (Customers) has a
   City/Municipality column (the name as on the list, or "Name, Province").

2. Forms & Proformas > a form > Edit: the Signatory and Position, kept the same
 - When the name in the Signatory box is a signature of the page, the two are linked: a
   change in Signatory or Position changes that signature in the page, and a change in
   the page (or in the Signatories part of the panel) changes the boxes. The note under
   the boxes says which signatory of the page they follow.
 - A new form takes its Position from the page: the omnibus sworn statement shows
   "Affiant" (as printed under the name), no longer the position of Contracting Settings.

3. Undo and Redo on every form and template
 - The arrows on the editor's toolbar, the same pair on the Fill in the blanks panel,
   Ctrl+Z (Undo) and Ctrl+Y or Ctrl+Shift+Z (Redo), and the browser's Edit > Undo.
 - They take back everything changed on the page, step by step: text typed in the page,
   a blank filled in or cleared, a signatory's name or position, the Signatory and
   Position boxes, and the gross amount, VAT and EWT of a disbursement voucher. Typing is
   taken back a few words at a time. The steps last until the page is closed.
 - Proforma & Form Templates > Edit the text has the same Undo and Redo.
 - A blank filled in now stays a field: it is still on the Fill in the blanks list with
   what was typed, so a mistake is corrected there (or by clicking it in the page), and
   clearing the box makes it blank again. No more "Fill again from the records" to fix a
   typing mistake. The same field used twice in the page changes in both places. Filled
   fields are lightly underlined while editing; the underline does not print.

4. The IAR's purchase order no. and date filled from the purchase order
 - What is filled into a blank and saved is kept for the transaction and fills the same
   blank on its other forms: the PO no. and date typed on the purchase order fill
   [Purchase order no.] and [Purchase order date] on the inspection and acceptance report.
 - An IAR made after the PO is saved has them at once. An IAR made before shows them filled
   on opening, with "Filled from the transaction's earlier forms: Purchase order no.
   (BFM-00031), Purchase order date (BFM-00031). Save to keep, or Undo to take them back."
 - Works for every field filled into a blank (IAR no., RIS no., fund, inspector ...). Not
   carried: dates of the day, the DV amounts, and the fields of a delivery, billing or
   collection (each form has its own). The transaction's own records come first. A field
   made blank again on the form it came from is no longer carried.

5. The signatories in Fill in the blanks
 - Under the blanks, "Signatories" lists every signature of the page with a Name and a
   Position box: the IAR's Inspection Officer / Inspection Committee and Supply and/or
   Property Custodian, the purchase order, the four of the requisition and issue slip, the
   six of the disbursement voucher (A, B, C, D, prepared by, approved by), the transaction
   form, the statements, the contracts, letters and receipts, and every form whose
   signatories are set on the template's Format tab.
 - Typing there changes the page; clicking a box outlines the signature it changes. A
   name that is a blank (the IAR's [Inspector]) is filled as that blank, everywhere.

The User's Manual (Customers > City/Municipality List and the Customer Center fields;
Barangay Contracts > Forms & Proformas > Undo and redo, filled fields and signatories)
explains it, with two new pictures.
