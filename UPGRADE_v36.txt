G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 36 (2026-11-24)
=========================================================================================

New billing pack "locked": every line with Available 0 and the message "Enter the quantity to
bill on at least one line (accepted quantities only)".

Why it happened
---------------
A billing pack holds its quantities from Prepare billing until its invoice is issued (Draft,
For review, Reviewed, Approved or Returned), so the same quantities are never billed twice.
The contract already had such a pack for everything accepted. The page did not say so:
"billed so far" counted only invoiced packs (0.00), the column read "Billed (others)", and
the Process tab kept offering "Make the billing pack" until the invoice was issued, which
led back to the page where nothing could be entered.

What to do with a contract in that state (any version): Barangay Contracts > Billing Packs >
Status "Being prepared / in approval" > open the contract's pack > Send for review, Review,
Approve, Issue the invoice. To bill the quantities differently, Edit the pack (draft or
returned) or Void it.

This package holds ONLY the files that changed since version 35, in the same folders. Copy
them over your installation (folder government-contracting-system); every other file stays
as it is.

Upgrading an installation of version 35
---------------------------------------
1. Back up the program folder.
2. Copy the files of this package over your installation. config/config.php is not in the
   package; yours stays.
3. No migration: the database does not change.
4. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser.

What is new
-----------
Barangay Contracts > Billing Packs > New billing pack
 - At the top, "Billing pack BBL-..... is not invoiced yet": each pack of the contract not
   invoiced yet, with a link, its status, its amount and what it waits for (send it for
   review; the checker's review; the approver; issue its invoice; correct it and send it
   again).
 - The figures read: contract, invoiced so far, in billing packs not invoiced yet, accepted
   not billed.
 - The column "Billed (others)" is now "In other packs".
 - When nothing is left to bill, the bottom bar has "Open BBL-....." (one pack) and Cancel,
   with no Prepare billing and no Everything accepted. With nothing accepted and no pack
   waiting, the page says a quantity is billed once its delivery is accepted.
 - Saving with nothing left to bill names the pack: "Nothing is left to bill on BCP-.....:
   the accepted quantities are in billing pack BBL-..... (Draft), not invoiced yet. ..."
Projects & Contracts > a transaction > Process tab (step Prepare billing documents)
 - The Sales invoice row shows the amount in packs not invoiced yet and "Open BBL-....." for
   each; "Make the billing pack" shows only for accepted quantities that are in no pack.
Projects & Contracts > a contract
 - The figure Billed and the "Contract in force" line show the amount in packs not invoiced
   yet.
The User's Manual (Barangay Contracts > Billing Packs) explains it.
Nothing changes in what can be billed, in the approval of a pack or in the books.
