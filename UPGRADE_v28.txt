G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 28 (2026-11-10)
=========================================================================================

Every proforma and form now has a letterhead with its logos and its signatories that are
set on the Format tab, the papers of steps 8 and 13 of the bidding follow the company's
own forms, and the transaction types Cash Out (purchases) and Kaliwaan (purchases) are
added.

This package holds ONLY the files that are new or changed since version 27, in the
same folders. Copy them over your installation (folder government-contracting-system);
every other file stays as it is.

Upgrading an installation of version 27
---------------------------------------
1. Back up the database and the program folder (storage/ and config/config.php too).
2. Copy the files of this package over your installation. config/config.php is not
   in the package; yours stays.
3. Run the new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database > Import;
   Navicat: Execute SQL File):

       database/migrations/2026_11_10_letterheads_bid_forms_types.sql

   It needs 2026_11_03_barangay_sop_forms.sql (version 27) to have been run first.
   With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies >
   Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page
   naming it.
4. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the changed assets/js/brgy.js and
   assets/css/app.css.

The migration adds the column txn_types to brgy_sop_steps and brgy_sop_papers, the
settings of the representative's title and cellphone, the person managing the firm
and the liaison officer, the types Cash Out (purchases) and Kaliwaan (purchases) before
the types already listed, one kind of document and one paper (notice of
post-qualification, in step 13), three templates (supplier profile, technical
specifications, notice of post-qualification) and the X marks of the two new types on
the pre-printed transaction form. The nine papers of steps 8 and 13 get their new
design as a new version; the other templates supplied with the program get the
{{letterhead}} and {{signatories}} fields in place of their fixed header and signatures,
as a new version, ONLY when their text was never changed (a template you changed keeps
its text: put the fields in with the buttons of its Format tab). Every version before
stays under Versions and can be put back. Nothing is deleted and no amount changes.
Written for MySQL 5.5 (no JSON functions, every column added through a check of
information_schema), safe to run twice.

New installation
----------------
Use the full version 26 package, copy the version 27 package and then this package over
it, then import ONE of the two database files of this package in phpMyAdmin (Import):

    database/government_contracting_system.sql        demo company with data
    database/government_contracting_system_clean.sql  setup and users, no transactions

Both already include this version. Log in as admin / Admin@12345 (a new password is
asked at once). Do NOT import those two files over a live database: they drop every
table.

What is new
-----------
 1. Letterhead and logos (Proforma & Form Templates > a template > Format). The field
    {{letterhead}} prints:
      - the company letterhead: the company logo and the name in a gold banner with the
        address and telephone (the look of the company's letters), or
      - the barangay letterhead: the seal of the city / municipality and the seal of the
        barangay on each side of Republic of the Philippines, the province, the city and
        the barangay (each barangay's own seals and names), or
      - a letterhead of the template's own, or none.
    The company and barangay letterheads are shared: a change on one template (the lines
    of text, pictures left and right, two pictures of your own uploaded in the box, the
    banner and its colours, the font, sizes, the line under it) shows on every template
    that uses it. Contracting Settings shows both and the templates using them.
 2. Signatories. The field {{signatories}} prints the signatures set on the Format tab:
    each with the words above it (Very truly yours, Conforme:, Received by:), the name,
    the position, more lines, bold / underlined / capitals / italics, a signature line,
    left / center / right / the right half of the page; how many side by side and the
    room to sign. Ready-made ones: company representative, conforme, received by,
    Punong Barangay, BAC Chairman, BAC members 1 to 5, treasurer, inspector, dealer's
    signature, a blank to write on.
 3. The Format tab also sets a footer after the text or at the bottom of the page (its
    alignment, size, bold), the colour of the table headings, the words of the statement
    of compliance, how dates are written (14 October 2026 or October 14, 2026), and the
    fonts CG Omega and Cambria.
 4. Step 8 of the bidding (additional requirements), from the company's forms, on Legal
    paper with one-inch margins: statement of all ongoing government and private
    contracts (filled from the company's awarded contracts, with % accomplished and the
    outstanding works), statement of the single largest completed similar contract
    (SLCC, from the closed contracts), supplier profile, letter of intent (manifestation
    of intent to participate), technical specifications (Section VII, with the statement
    of compliance), production details (Section VI, schedule of requirements, with the
    delivery period), manpower requirements, after-sales service and warranty provision.
    The specifications of each item of the price schedule print one a line; a line in
    capitals or ending with ":" prints bold, **text** is bold.
 5. Step 13 (post-bidding), prepared for the barangay's signatures with the barangay
    letterhead: notice of post-qualification (new), BAC resolution (BAC Chairman, five
    members and the Punong Barangay), notice of award and notice to proceed, with the
    contract price in words and figures and "OFFICE OF THE BARANGAY" at the foot.
 6. New fields: the representative as addressed (MR. JUAN DELA CRUZ) and in a salutation
    (Mr. J. Dela Cruz), the person managing the firm and the liaison officer, the
    barangay's name and city line (Barangay Sabang, City of Lapu-Lapu), BAC members 1 to
    5, the contract price and the ABC in words and figures, the delivery period and the
    warranty in words, the location of the project, the procurement law in a sentence,
    today's date written either way, and the statement tables.
 7. Transaction types Cash Out (purchases) and Kaliwaan (purchases) on the new
    transaction form (Contracting Settings > The SOP lists them); the transaction form
    prints CASH OUT and KALIWAAN boxes, and the pre-printed pad has their X marks.
    SOP Steps & Papers: a step or paper can be limited to some types of transaction;
    a transaction of another type skips the step (or marks the paper not needed) with a
    note saying why, and gets it back when its type changes.

Files
-----
 - storage/contracts/ also holds the letterhead pictures (lhpic_*). They are shown to
   logged-in users through index.php?p=file.lhpic. Include the folder in file backups.
 - php tools/test_brgy.php --yes also checks the letterheads, signatories, the new forms
   and the types of transaction (on a TEST COPY of a database only).
 - tests/selenium: the sop suite has new cases for the types, step 8, the letterhead and
   signatories of a template, and the Format tab on a phone.

The user's manual, chapter 11, has the new sections Letterheads, logos and signatories,
and The papers of steps 8 and 13.
