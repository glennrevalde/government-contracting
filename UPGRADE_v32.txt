G-SMaRT Integrated Accounting and Government Contracting System - version 32 (2026-11-19)
=========================================================================================

Accounting updates. The accounting features of G-SMaRT Accounting (its versions 26 to 42)
are brought into this system: comparative statements, reports by class, Print and Save
as PDF on every report, card and e-wallet settlements on Make Deposits, a supplier on
expense lines, attachments, voucher signatures, item group pricing and the inventory
costing fixes. They were adapted to this system's files, not copied over them, so
Barangay Contracts, the several-company platform, the installable app (version 30) and
the New company fix (version 31) keep working as before.

This package holds ONLY the files that are new or changed since version 31, in the
same folders. Copy them over your installation (folder government-contracting-system);
every other file stays as it is.

Upgrading an installation of version 31
---------------------------------------
1. Back up the database and the program folder (storage/ and config/config.php too).
2. Copy the files of this package over your installation. config/config.php is not
   in the package; yours stays.
3. Run the one new migration on your database (phpMyAdmin: select the database >
   Import; Navicat: Execute SQL File):

       database/migrations/2026_11_19_accounting_updates.sql

   With several companies, run it on every company database (Super Admin: Companies >
   Database updates > Update all companies). Until it is run, the program shows a page
   naming it. It is safe to run a second time.
4. Log in as the administrator and run System Administration > Utilities:
   Verify & Rebuild Data > Rebuild data once, so the average costs of the past
   inventory transactions follow the new costing (step 4 of the migration explains why).
5. Press Ctrl+F5 once in the browser so it loads the changed assets/css/app.css,
   assets/js/app.js and the new assets/js/signature.js.
6. Optional, only if people outside your office will sign vouchers through a link: the
   link starts with the address the program was opened with. Open the program through
   its public address before making links, or add this line inside the 'app' settings
   of config/config.php:

       'public_url' => 'https://accounts.example.com/government-contracting-system',

A new installation needs no migration: database/schema.sql, the installer and the two
importable files (database/government_contracting_system.sql with the sample data,
database/government_contracting_system_clean.sql without transactions) already hold
everything.

What the migration does
-----------------------
 - Item groups: an empty Price variance GL account column on items, five empty group
   columns on transaction_lines, the table item_group_sales, the account 4070 Item Group
   Price Variance (Income; the next free code if 4070 is taken) with its module default
   account, and the report Sales by Item Group.
 - Receiving lists: an empty column received_via on transactions, filled in for the item
   receipts and the bills entered with Receive Items and Enter Bills.
 - The eight comparative income statements and balance sheets, Trial Balance by Class
   and Statement of Cash Flows by Class, given to the roles that may already open the
   Income Statement, the Balance Sheet, the Trial Balance and the Statement of Cash Flows.
 - Average cost on the books: each quantity on hand by site is set to the total of the
   inventory movements at that site, and each average cost to the remaining value
   divided by the remaining quantity. These are the figures the program works out
   from the movements; no transaction, ledger line or amount posted changes. Plus the
   report Average Cost Changes, given to the roles that open Inventory Valuation Detail.
 - The tables clearing_settlements (card and e-wallet receipts ticked on a deposit),
   txn_attachments and txn_attachment_chunks (attached files), voucher_sign_requests,
   voucher_signatures, voucher_documents and voucher_document_chunks (signatures and
   the signed PDF copies).
 - One line in the audit trail saying the migration was run.
Nothing is deleted. Users, roles and the log-in are not changed: the new reports are
only given to the roles that already open the matching report.

What is new
-----------
Chart of Accounts > Reports (the Financial Reports card of the Report Center)
 - Comparative Income Statement by Month, by Quarter, by Year and by Custom Range;
   Comparative Balance Sheet by Month, by Quarter, by Year and by Custom Date. One
   column per period, a Total and the change from the period before; click an amount
   for its entries in the General Ledger.
 - Trial Balance by Class and Statement of Cash Flows by Class.
 - General Ledger: a Cashier filter.
Every report
 - Print and Save as PDF side by side; the print page has Print, Save as PDF and Back.
 - Sort by (Increasing or Decreasing) on Income by Customer Summary, Sales by Item
   Summary, Inventory Sales by Site and Sales by Item Group.
 - Home > Report Center: Find a report by name.
Banking
 - Make Deposits lists the journal entries to Undeposited Funds with the payments: a
   debit adds to the deposit, a credit deducts from it.
 - Card and e-wallet settlements: Unsettled is the balance of the clearing account in
   the General Ledger on the deposit date (the same as the Trial Balance). Details
   explains each balance (what it is made of, the sales by payment method, the receipts
   not yet settled and the latest settlements) and lets you tick Settle on the receipts
   the provider paid; their total goes into Gross settled.
 - The POS: a card or e-wallet payment taken on account now goes to its clearing
   account, like a card sale.
 - Write Checks, Cash Voucher and Enter Bills: the Expenses tab has Customer / Supplier.
   A supplier puts the line on that supplier's subsidiary ledger.
Vouchers and documents
 - Sign Voucher and Request Signature on check vouchers, cash vouchers and journal
   entries (Signatures box on the voucher page). A signature link (sign.php) works once,
   for one line, without an account. Every signature saves a new signed PDF; the
   original unsigned voucher is kept. A signed voucher cannot be edited, voided or
   deleted until a user with the Delete/Void right resets its signatures.
 - Attachments on journal entries, checks, cash vouchers, bills, purchase orders, sales
   orders and invoices: on the form and on the page of the saved document, with a paper
   clip in the lists. Files are kept in the database, so Backup & Restore carries them.
Item groups
 - The form of a group has Group price, Consolidated price, Price variance and the
   Price variance GL account, and Sold as a group at its foot.
 - On estimates, sales orders, invoices, sales receipts, statement charges and credit
   memos the group is one line at its group price; its items are recorded at their own
   prices plus one price variance line. The invoice page has View by item group and
   View item details.
 - Report Sales by Item Group (Customers > Reports).
 - Settings (Company, Security & Closing Date > Inventory and POS): Track Item Group
   Sales transactions, Adopt the Group Price in the Item Group.
Inventory costing
 - Stock sold below zero is settled at the cost of the goods received for it.
 - The average cost is always the remaining value divided by the remaining quantity.
 - A back-dated, edited, voided or deleted inventory transaction costs the later ones
   again, in date order.
 - Report Average Cost Changes; the Inventory Center has the transaction history of the
   item (Latest 100 / 500 / All, per site) and an Average cost changes button.
 - Settings: Block sales of items with no unit cost; Block posting processing orders
   with no unit cost; Disassemble / Process Items: largest difference allowed between
   the cost of the outputs and of the inputs (2%).
Vendors
 - Receipt Management has a Receipt List; Receive Items and Receive Items and Enter
   Bills each have the list of what was entered on them.
The User's Manual has the sections of every new page and report (Help on each page).

Left out on purpose
-------------------
These parts of G-SMaRT Accounting are not in this update: the POS changes (FB POS,
Cash Count, item groups sold at the POS and POS Sales by Item Group, Ending Inventory -
POS Cashier, dates with times and shifts on the inventory movement reports), Community,
the Add new entries in the drop-downs, the renaming of the Chart of Accounts menu to
Financial, and the Exclude zero filters of the inventory reports. The only POS change is
the clearing account of card and e-wallet payments on account, because it is an
accounting fix.

Member-related integrations: this system has none, and this update adds none.
